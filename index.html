Overview

Two workload paradigms

The established n-back test and a novel simulator-based k-drive task induce single- and dual-task load of increasing intensity, captured on a common analog clock alongside gaze, facial action units and driving performance.

ADABase is continued by REVELIO, a gaming cohort recorded with the same setup. Together they comprise 96 sessions from 83 unique participants.
